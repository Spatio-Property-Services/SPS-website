Consultancy Services

Professional Property Consultancy

End-to-end support for property transactions, legal documentation, land processes, and auctioneering across Kenya.

Trusted Advisory for Property & Land Matters

We simplify complex real estate and land administration processes through our network of licensed legal, surveying, and auctioneering partners — ensuring compliance, speed, and peace of mind.

Our Consultancy Services Include:

  • End-to-end Ardhi Sasa registration support.
  • Official land registry searches and title verification
  • Property transaction advisory (buying, selling, leasing)
  • Land title verification and due diligence
  • Processing of transfers at Ardhi House
  • Subdivision, amalgamation and change of user applications
  • Representation in land disputes and boundary conflicts
  • Property auctioneering and valuation support
  • Guidance on physical planning and NEMA requirements

Why Partner With Us?

  • Direct access to registered advocates, surveyors, and auctioneers
  • Transparent timelines and cost breakdowns
  • No hidden fees — fixed or hourly packages available
  • Dedicated case manager from start to completion

Our Commitment

  • Legal Compliance — All processes follow Kenyan land laws and regulations.
  • Expert Network — Vetted professionals across Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu & more.
  • Time-Saving — Avoid long queues and bureaucratic delays.
  • Client-Centric — We act in your best interest, always.
